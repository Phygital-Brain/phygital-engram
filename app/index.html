How the ranking works
  1. Notes with your words come first. Rare words count more than common ones.
  2. Versions, IDs, error text and "quoted words" also match letter for letter, and get a small boost.
  3. Rule files (CLAUDE.md), Context and decisions never fade. Other notes fade slowly with age.
  4. Archives sit on a back shelf and only fill in after your notes.
  5. Locked private notes show where a match is, never the text, until you open them.
  6. Saved AI conversations come after your notes and fade faster.
